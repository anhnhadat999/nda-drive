AINO PROXY - cho trinh duyet Sony Ericsson Aino (U10i) luot web hien nay
=========================================================================

Proxy chay tren may tinh Windows. Dien thoai noi Wi-Fi cung mang nha, tro proxy ve may tinh.
Proxy se: mo cac trang https hien nay thay dien thoai, bo JavaScript/CSS nang, thu nho anh,
chia trang dai thanh nhieu trang nho, va ghi lai moi truy van vao thu muc "logs".

CAI DAT (1 lan)
1. Cai Python 3 cho Windows: https://www.python.org/downloads/
   -> Khi cai NHO TICK o "Add python.exe to PATH".
2. Giai nen thu muc nay vao dau do (vd: C:\AinoProxy).
3. Bam dup "CHAY-PROXY.bat".
   - Lan dau se tu cai them Pillow (de thu nho anh).
   - Windows hoi tuong lua (Firewall): tick "Private networks / Mang rieng" -> Allow.
4. Cua so den hien:  Dia chi proxy : 192.168.x.x   Cong : 8080
   -> Ghi lai 2 so nay. De cua so mo trong luc dung.

TREN DIEN THOAI AINO
1. Ket noi Wi-Fi nha (cung mang voi may tinh).
2. Vao cai dat Wi-Fi cua mang do (Cai dat > Ket noi > Wi-Fi > chon mang > Cai dat nang cao/Proxy):
   - Bat proxy, Dia chi = so 192.168.x.x o tren, Cong = 8080.
   (Neu khong thay muc proxy trong Wi-Fi: vao Cai dat > Ket noi > Cai dat Internet > tai khoan
    Internet dang dung > Cai dat proxy.)
3. Mo trinh duyet, vao:  http://aino/
   -> Trang chu co o tim kiem, lien ket nhanh (Wikipedia, bao, thoi tiet).
4. Go dia chi bat ky BANG http:// (vd http://vnexpress.net) - proxy tu mo ban https.

MEO
- Trang chu > "Anh: bat/tat" de tat anh cho tai nhanh hon.
- Trang dai co nut "Sau >>" o tren va duoi.
- Dang nhap tai khoan qua proxy: chi nen dung voi trang khong quan trong (proxy chay tren may
  nha anh nen an toan trong mang nha, nhung trang cu khong co ma hoa giua dien thoai va may tinh).
- Proxy chi nhan ket noi tu mang nha (192.168.x.x), nguoi ngoai khong dung ke duoc.

THU MUC logs
- requests-*.log : moi trang dien thoai da mo.
- services-*.log : truy van cua ung dung/dich vu he thong (khong phai trinh duyet), kem noi dung.
  Day la du lieu de dung lai server cho cac tinh nang cu (thoi tiet, TrackID, PlayNow...).
  Muon lam tiep: mo tung tinh nang tren may khi dang dung proxy, roi gui file services-*.log cho Claude.
